# Asset provenance and visual-use policy

This file records the origin and intended license of visual assets shipped with
Lociqua. It is an engineering record, not legal advice or trademark clearance.

## Current assets

| Asset | Creation method | Purpose | License |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `lociqua-mark.png` | Existing project-provided mark; manual ownership review required before public use | Application mark | Apache-2.0, subject to ownership review |
| `lociqua-readme-hero.png` | Existing project-provided illustration; manual ownership review required before public use | Legacy README hero | Apache-2.0, subject to ownership review |
| `lociqua-trusted-data-flow.png` | Existing project-provided illustration; manual ownership review required before public use | Legacy data-rights illustration | Apache-2.0, subject to ownership review |
| `diagrams/*.svg` | Repository-authored SVG shapes, labels, and colors | Documentation diagrams | Apache-2.0 |

The SVG diagrams contain no external URLs, scripts, fonts, raster images,
tracking, third-party logos, or embedded data. They are intentionally generic
and can be edited with a text editor.

The unreferenced byte-identical legacy duplicate of `lociqua-mark.png` was
removed during the documentation refresh.

## Review required before adding an asset

- Confirm that the contributor created it or has a license that permits the
  repository's intended use and redistribution.
- Record its origin, license, and required attribution in this file.
- Do not add copied artwork, screenshots of third-party products, stock assets
  without a compatible license, or another organization's logo or trademark.
- Do not embed remote images, web fonts, analytics, executable SVG content, or
  hidden metadata.
- Treat AI-generated artwork as requiring human review for confusing similarity
  to known people, brands, logos, or copyrighted artwork.

The Apache-2.0 license applies to repository contributions. It does not grant
rights to third-party data, names, brands, privacy-sensitive content, or source
contracts.
