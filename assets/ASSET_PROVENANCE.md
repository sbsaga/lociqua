## Review required before adding an asset

- Confirm that the contributor created it or has a license that permits the
  repository's intended use and redistribution.
- Record its origin, license, and required attribution in this file.
- Do not add copied artwork, screenshots of third-party products, stock assets
  without a compatible license, or another organization's logo or trademark.
- Do not embed remote images, web fonts, analytics, executable SVG content, or
  hidden metadata.
- Treat AI-generated artwork as requiring human review for confusing similarity
  to known people, brands, logos, or copyrighted artwork.

The Apache-2.0 license applies to repository contributions. It does not grant
rights to third-party data, names, brands, privacy-sensitive content, or source
contracts.
