# Lociqua use cases

![Authorized data flow from source to controlled business decisions](../assets/diagrams/authorized-data-flow.svg)

Lociqua helps teams organize business records they are permitted to use. It is
not a global directory, a lead-generation guarantee, or a way to collect data
from restricted sources.

## Agencies and research teams

An agency can import a customer-approved CSV, search several service areas, and
export only the records needed for a permitted project. Source metadata makes it
clear where each record came from.

## Internal sales and operations teams

A team can turn a CRM export or an approved partner list into a searchable
directory. Quality views expose missing fields; duplicate review captures a
human decision without deleting either record.

## Franchise and location research

Teams evaluating locations can run one bounded search per locality and compare
results independently. This avoids waiting for one location's work before
viewing another's results.

## Data stewardship

Data owners can record the permission basis for each source, inspect provenance
by field, view audit history, and limit access through viewer, editor, and owner
roles.

## A realistic outcome

Lociqua improves the organization and traceability of authorized data already
available to a team. Its output quality depends on the source data, configured
permissions, and the team's review process.
