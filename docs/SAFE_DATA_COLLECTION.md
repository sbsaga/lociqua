# Safe data collection boundaries

Lociqua is designed for authorised business-data operations. This page explains
the product boundary in plain language; it is not legal advice.

## Lociqua supports

- Importing files your organisation owns or is permitted to use.
- Connecting authorised, operator-configured feeds or official APIs when their
  terms, licence, attribution, retention, and storage rules are recorded.
- Human-confirmed capture from a website approved by an owner.
- Provenance, audit history, source policy, review, retention, and export
  controls.

## Lociqua does not support

- Automated collection from restricted websites.
- Search-engine or mapping-service result harvesting.
- CAPTCHA solving, proxy rotation, device or browser fingerprint evasion, or
  bypassing bot protections.
- Credential collection, session replay, or accessing pages behind a login
  without the operator's authority.
- Claims that the directory is complete, globally accurate, legally compliant,
  or suitable for unrestricted outreach.

## Why this boundary is useful

Teams need more than a large list of leads. They need to know whether a field is
recent, whether it can be exported, whether attribution is required, and which
person reviewed it. Lociqua makes those questions part of the record instead of
leaving them in a spreadsheet or someone’s memory.
