# Web research playbook

This guide explains how a non-technical team can use Lociqua to organise
business research responsibly. It assumes your organisation has already decided
which sources it may use.

![Approved sources pass a policy gate while unapproved sources are kept out](../assets/lociqua-approved-source-governance.png)

## The simple idea

Lociqua is not a tool that searches every website for you. It is a place to
keep approved research organised after a person has looked at a permitted
source. The useful result is not only a company name or phone number; it is the
answer to: **where did this information come from, who saved it, and can we use
or export it?**

## Before anyone captures a record

An owner creates an approved source policy. A good policy answers these
questions:

| Question | Example answer |
| --- | --- |
| Which source? | `directory.example.com` |
| Why may we use it? | Partner agreement or an open-data licence |
| How may it be used? | Human-confirmed browser capture only |
| Which fields are allowed? | Name, website, city, phone |
| Can results be exported? | Yes, with attribution; or no |
| How long may evidence remain? | 90 days |

Do not approve a source simply because it is publicly visible. Public access is
not automatically permission to collect, retain, contact, export, or resell
data.

## Daily workflow for a researcher

1. Sign in to Lociqua.
2. Create or open a research session for the business type and locations.
3. Open an approved source using your normal browser.
4. Click the optional Lociqua extension.
5. Select only the fields that you have reviewed and are allowed to save.
6. Check the preview and confirm the capture.
7. Let an editor or owner review the evidence when required.

![A researcher explicitly selects fields, reviews a preview, and confirms a single evidence capture](../assets/lociqua-human-evidence-capture.png)

## What happens after capture

The record carries evidence rather than losing its history. Lociqua preserves
the source URL, source policy, capture time, user, selected fields, and review
state. If a policy has a retention period, the worker later removes expired
evidence while leaving the company record available for normal quality review.

![Evidence moves from source link to human review, audited record, and retention point](../assets/lociqua-evidence-lifecycle.png)

## What to do when something is uncertain

- **The domain is not approved:** stop. Ask an owner to review and create a
  source policy before capture.
- **A field seems wrong:** mark evidence for correction or reject it; do not
  silently overwrite a company record.
- **Two companies may be the same:** use duplicate review. Approval records a
  decision and never deletes either original record.
- **An export is blocked:** respect the source policy. Ask the policy owner to
  review the permission basis; do not bypass the restriction.
