# Data rights and responsible use

![Authorized sources flow into a protected local store and searchable records](../assets/lociqua-trusted-data-flow.png)

Lociqua is designed for data you own, data a user has permission to provide, or
data available under a license that permits the intended use.

## Good sources

- Your organization’s CRM export.
- A customer-provided company list with permission.
- Your own directory or internal database.
- A licensed provider, only within its contract.
- Permitted open data, with required attribution and license compliance.

## Not supported

- CAPTCHA solving or access-control bypass.
- Proxy or account rotation to evade limits.
- Scraping a source that prohibits collection.
- Rehosting data when the source license prohibits it.

The Apache-2.0 software license applies to this code. It does **not** grant
rights to collect, store, redistribute, or contact people using third-party data.
For OpenStreetMap data, follow its ODbL license and attribution requirements.

This document is practical guidance, not legal advice. Obtain legal review for
commercial use, sensitive personal data, or a new jurisdiction.
